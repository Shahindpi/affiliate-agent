# Content mix

1. Open **Affiliate Agent → Automation**. Set Tutorials 30, Educational 25, Use cases 20, Tips 15, Promotion 10 (or your own percentages totalling exactly 100).
2. Set a rolling horizon, then save. Values outside 0–100 or a total other than 100 are rejected.
3. The planner compares the desired share in its rolling horizon with existing content counts, choosing the largest shortfall. For instance, if promotion is already over target, the next candidate favors a category with a deficit.
4. Keep multiple eligible brands/products enabled for variety. An active campaign can prioritize its brand/product until the campaign monthly target is reached. Check **Content & history** after generation and adjust percentages if needed. This allocation is a planning target; source/media availability and failed jobs affect actual totals.
